## 👋 About Me

Hey! I'm **Razin**, a full-stack developer from Malaysia with **5 years of coding experience**.
My home is the **backend**: APIs, databases, security, and the business logic that keeps a
company running. For UI/UX I team up with a dedicated frontend designer, so every product looks as
good as it works.
